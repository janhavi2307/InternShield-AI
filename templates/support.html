Quick help

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about InternShield's assessment and tracking workflows.

Why can a high stipend still receive a warning?

Compensation is only one signal. Verification, recruiter evidence, workload, consistency and warning indicators are evaluated separately.

Does InternShield prove a company is legitimate?

No. InternShield organizes evidence and highlights what should be verified independently before accepting.

What happens when a final offer changes?

Offer Change Detection compares the final written offer against the original assessed opportunity.

Is my Application Tracker private?

Tracker records are associated with your signed-in account and protected by the project's Supabase access rules.
